🤝 Block 1

Key Partners

  • Local flower farms & nurseries supplying fresh-cut flowers daily
  • Certified bakeries and waffle ingredient suppliers
  • Razorpay — payment gateway partner
  • MongoDB Atlas — cloud database hosting
  • Delivery & logistics partners for last-mile delivery
  • Email service provider (Gmail / Nodemailer) for order notifications
  • Social media influencers for marketing campaigns

⚙️ Block 2

Key Activities

  • Sourcing and quality-checking fresh flowers daily
  • Baking and preparing waffles fresh each morning
  • Managing the e-commerce platform (products, orders)
  • Processing payments and managing order fulfilment
  • Running digital marketing campaigns (SEO, email)

🏗️ Block 3

Key Resources

  • E-commerce website (Node.js + MongoDB)
  • Product inventory (flowers + waffle ingredients)
  • Admin dashboard for product & order management
  • Customer database with login system
  • Delivery fleet / partner network

💎 Block 4

Value Proposition

  • Convenience: One platform to order both fresh flowers and freshly baked waffles online
  • Freshness: Flowers handpicked daily, waffles baked every morning — guaranteed freshness
  • Gifting: Perfect combination for gifting — a bouquet + a waffle box for any occasion
  • Transparency: Real-time order status tracking and email confirmations
  • Security: bcrypt-secured login, brute-force protection, Razorpay secure payments
  • Personalisation: Customer accounts with order history and tailored experience

💬 Block 5

Customer Relationships

  • Personalised customer accounts with order history
  • Automated welcome & order confirmation emails
  • Real-time order status (Pending → Processing → Delivered)
  • SEO-driven content to attract organic traffic

📡 Block 6

Channels

  • Direct website: flowersandwaffles.com
  • Email marketing (order emails, promotions)
  • Social media (Instagram, WhatsApp)
  • Google Search (SEO-optimised pages)

👥 Block 7

Customer Segments

  • Gift buyers — looking for unique flower + waffle gift combos for birthdays, anniversaries
  • Waffle lovers — food enthusiasts who want fresh gourmet waffles delivered
  • Flower lovers — people who regularly buy fresh flowers for home or gifting
  • Corporate clients — offices ordering in bulk for events or employee gifting
  • Health-conscious — customers interested in healthy waffle options

💸 Block 8

Cost Structure

  • Flower sourcing and procurement costs
  • Waffle ingredients and bakery operations
  • Website hosting and cloud database (MongoDB Atlas)
  • Payment gateway transaction fees (Razorpay ~2%)
  • Delivery and logistics costs
  • Digital marketing and SEO
  • Staff salaries (bakers, delivery, admin)
  • Email service and notification system

💰 Block 9

Revenue Streams

  • Product sales — direct revenue from flower and waffle orders
  • Gift bundles — premium pricing on flower + waffle combo packages
  • Bulk / corporate orders — higher volume at negotiated rates
  • Seasonal specials — premium pricing on Valentine's Day, Diwali, etc.
  • Delivery charges — nominal fee for same-day or express delivery
